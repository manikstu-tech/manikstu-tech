What We Do

Programs That Build & Capability

From the field to the federation, our training programs meet farmers where they are and grow with them.

Farmer Field Training

Hands-on training at our model farms covering goat husbandry, feeding, healthcare and farm management.

FPO & SHG Capacity Building

Strengthening Farmer Producer Organizations and Self-Help Groups with governance, accounting and enterprise skills.

Veterinary & Animal Health Awareness

Community vet camps and awareness drives on vaccination, deworming and disease prevention.

Sustainable & Regenerative Farming

Fodder cultivation, water stewardship and organic practices for long-term farm sustainability.

Women & Youth Empowerment

Skill development and entrepreneurship pathways for rural women and young people.

Goat Bank & Livelihood Training

Training on our Goat Bank model — from animal selection to repayment and herd management.

Awareness Initiatives

Reaching Every Village & Household

Awareness is the foundation of adoption. We bring knowledge directly to communities through village drives, health camps and local media.

Community Awareness Drives

Village-level campaigns on nutrition, hygiene and livestock best practices.

Veterinary Health Camps

Periodic camps for vaccination, deworming and general health check-ups.

Demo Plots & Field Days

Live demonstrations of fodder, feeding techniques and sustainable farming methods.

Rural Radio & IVRS Advisories

Timely, localized advisories on weather, market prices and animal health via radio and phone.
