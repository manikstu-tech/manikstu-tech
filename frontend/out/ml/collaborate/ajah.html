Project AJAH weaves together the full livelihood stack — from animal genetics to market access — so women farmers can build sustainable, dignified incomes.

Scientific Livestock Management

Improved breeding, feeding and husbandry practices for higher productivity.

Improved Infrastructure

Better housing, fodder storage and water systems for healthier herds.

Animal Healthcare

Routine vaccination, disease prevention and veterinary access.

Training

Insurance

Market Support

Linkages to fair, reliable markets for goat products and poultry.
